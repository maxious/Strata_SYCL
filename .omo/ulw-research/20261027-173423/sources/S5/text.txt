Purpose
Int4 MoE decode on Xe2 spends most of its expert time in the fixed cost of . At one decode token with top-8 routing each expert sees one row. On Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (GPTQ int4 experts), the 8 active experts of a layer are 13 MB of weights, about 21 us at the B70's bandwidth, but a gate_up call takes ~56 us and a down call ~34 us.
This PR adds , a SYCL GEMV for the int4 layout the grouped GEMM already uses (int8-packed signed 4-bit from , per-group scales in the activation dtype). routes int4 calls with at most 8 rows to it (); larger calls (prefill, MTP verify, batched decode) keep the grouped GEMM. turns it off.
Kernel: one work-group per (active expert, block of N). The work-group finds its expert with a group scan over (no host sync, works under graph capture). Each sub-group computes 4 output columns for up to 2 rows at a time, lanes split K in chunks of 32 int4 values (16-byte weight loads), fp32 accumulation, per-group scale applied per chunk.
Related: #509 (fixed per-step MoE decode cost on the Arc Pro B70).
Related, not duplicates: #628 / #637 (fused routed + shared-expert decode for FP8 per-tensor weights) and #623 (FP8 decode GEMV). This PR covers int4 experts in the existing path and does not touch those.
Test Plan
checks the op against a dequantized fp32 reference and against on the same inputs: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B shapes (w13 1024x2048, w2 2048x512) and 5120x8192, 16 and 256 experts, 1-8 rows with one or two rows per expert, bf16 and fp16.
E2E (vLLM main + this branch, one B70): (palmfuture), single request, decode tok/s with , plus a short accuracy eval (gsm8k via lm_eval, 250 questions) for both.
Test Result
Measured 2026-10-15 on one Arc Pro B70 (b70 tab, testq 004). Not run: a full build of upstream main 6b30c9b.
pytest (installed package + this branch):

The test now generates them as uint8 and runs that shape with 16 experts only, like the existing cases for it (256 experts would still need ~10 GiB), so its 30 cases are exactly the shapes that passed above (the uint8 generation itself has not been run on a GPU yet).
